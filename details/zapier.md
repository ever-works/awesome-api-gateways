## Overview

Zapier is an automation platform that lets you connect the apps you use every day to automate your work and be more productive. It supports 1000+ apps with easy integrations.

## Features

- Ease of Use: No-code visual workflow builder
- Wide Range of Integrations: 1000+ app connections
- Custom Automation: Multi-step workflows with conditional logic
- Scalability: Plans for individuals, teams, and enterprises
- App Triggers and Actions: Event-based automation between services

## Pricing

- Free: Limited tasks for basic automation
- Starter: Paid plan for individuals
- Professional: Advanced workflows for teams
- Team and Company: Enterprise plans with admin controls

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted SaaS platform.
